Hosting

This static website is hosted on Vercel. When you visit, the host processes technical log data (such as IP address, date and time, requested file) to deliver and secure the site. This website itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

External content

Videos load only after you click play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube (Google) may then process your data under its own policy. If the portfolio shows YouTube thumbnails, they are loaded from i.ytimg.com when the page opens, which transmits your IP address to Google.

Orders and payment

When you order, the details you enter (name, email, project description, links) are stored in a database (Supabase) to process your order. Payment is handled by Stripe, who receives the data needed for payment; card details are entered on Stripe's pages and never reach this website.
